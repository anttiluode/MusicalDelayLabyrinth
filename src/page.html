Musical Delay Labyrinth

Every corridor is a delay. A corridor's length is how long sound takes to walk it. Corridors whose echoes land on the beat turn gold, retime themselves to it and take more of the sound. Click a room to drop a pebble in; Walls opens or closes corridors; Gate moves where the song enters.
